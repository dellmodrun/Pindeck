LAUNCH SEQUENCE

1 signature
  1. 01picturePNG or JPEG sent over USB into the chip's flash; the badge makes the thumbnail
  2. 02namename, ticker, description: typed on your PC's keyboard, over USB
  3. 03ipfsthe picture, then the metadata, up with your own key
  4. 04press okit builds the create_v2, shows it, signs when you press OK

THIS BROWSER

devnet

Your wallet here is a devnet wallet in this browser's storage. Launching needs devnet SOL (faucet.solana.com) and an IPFS key (wallet ipfs key).

FIRMWARE

C under a Lua app
  • ✓walletEd25519 in C; the key stays in the badge's flash, apps only see the address
  • ✓approveevery signature waits for the OS's own dialog: nothing is signed until you OK it
  • ✓rpcJSON-RPC straight from the badge over Wi-Fi, no phone in the middle
  • ✓imagesPNG and JPEG sent over USB, decoded and thumbnailed on the chip
  • ✓ipfspicture and metadata go up with your own key, from the badge
  • ✓kbtext fields take your PC's keyboard and paste over USB
  • ✓mintbring a vanity mint made on your PC (…pump), or a fresh random one
